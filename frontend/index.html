A thoughtful guide, with clear boundaries

Replies use a local model and a local corpus. Citations show where passages came from; they do not prove an interpretation is correct. Source-faithfulness and qualified teaching, safeguarding and mental-health review are still required before student use.

If you feel unsafe or need urgent help, contact local emergency services or someone you trust directly. This chat cannot assess or respond to an emergency. Do not change treatment on its advice.

No chat is saved by this interface, and there are no accounts or analytics. The browser, operating system or local model service may behave differently; this is not a complete privacy audit. Source links open external sites only when you choose them.

Context is limited to four completed pairs, 1,000 characters per history message and 2,000 total. If a limit or failed response interrupts the context, you will be asked to start a new conversation rather than silently dropping earlier messages.
